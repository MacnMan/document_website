# How to Contact Macnman Support

Macnman support can be reached in three ways: chat, a support ticket, or email. Chat and tickets live in **Macnman Track**, the customer portal where you also follow your orders. This page explains when to use each one and how.

## Choose a Support Channel

| Channel | Best for | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Chat | Quick questions about an order, setup or configuration | Macnman Track, on each order |
| Support ticket | Faults, hardware issues and anything that needs an engineer | Macnman Track, on each order |
| Email | No Macnman Track account, RMA requests, formal communication | support@macnman.com |

:::tip Not sure which one to pick?
Start with chat. If the issue needs a detailed check, the support team will ask you to raise a ticket and send you a button that opens the form.
:::

## Before You Contact Support

Keep the following ready. The more accurate the information, the faster the diagnosis.

- Product name and model number  
- Serial number / QR code / MAC ID of the device  
- Order ID or invoice number  
- Firmware version (if applicable)  
- What is happening, since when and how often  
- Installation location (site name, city, project name)  
- Troubleshooting steps already tried  
- Photos, screenshots or log files  

## Sign In to Macnman Track

Macnman Track is the customer portal for order tracking, documents, chat and support tickets.

1. Open [Macnman Track](https://www.macnman.com/erp/login), or select **Login** at the top of macnman.com.  
2. Sign in with the email address registered on your order.  
3. If this is your first visit, select **Forgot Password**. An OTP is sent to your registered email, and you can then set your own password.  

Macnman creates your account with the email address on your order and sends you a welcome email. If you cannot sign in, contact your Macnman representative or write to **support@macnman.com**.

## Chat with the Support Team

Every order in Macnman Track has its own support chat. Use it for quick questions about that order.

1. Sign in to Macnman Track. Your orders are listed on the dashboard.  
2. Select **Chat** on the order you need help with. The same button is at the top of the order page after you select **Track order**.  
3. The chat opens in the bottom-right corner, or full screen on a phone. Type your message and press Enter.  
4. Add files with the paperclip button and photos with the image button. You can also paste a screenshot.  

Replies appear in the same window. If you close it, a red badge on the **Chat** button shows how many replies are unread. The full conversation is kept, so you can return to it at any time.

### What You Can Send in Chat

| Item | Limit |
|---|---|
| File types | PDF, JPG, PNG, WEBP, DOCX, XLSX |
| File size | Up to 4 MB per file |
| Attachments | Up to 5 per message |
| Message length | Up to 4,000 characters |

### During the Conversation

- The support team may ask whether your problem was solved. Answer **Satisfied** or **Not satisfied** so the team knows whether to continue.  
- If the issue needs a detailed check, the team sends a **Raise ticket** button in the chat. It opens the ticket form for that order.  
- Messages sent outside working hours are answered when the team is back online.  

:::note No Macnman Track account yet?
Use the chat bubble at the bottom-right of [macnman.com](https://www.macnman.com/) for general and pre-sales questions. Chat about a specific order takes place in Macnman Track.
:::

## Raise a Ticket from Macnman Track

Raise a ticket when a device is faulty, when an issue needs an engineer, or when you want the resolution tracked from start to finish.

1. Sign in to Macnman Track and select **Track order** on the affected order.  
2. Select **Raise ticket** at the top of the order page.  
3. Complete the troubleshooting checklist described below. The form unlocks after you open all four guides.  
4. Upload a PDF with photos, screenshots or details of the problem. This is optional and limited to one PDF of up to 4 MB.  
5. Choose your **support meeting availability**. Pick a date from Monday to Friday and a time between 10:00 AM and 5:00 PM IST.  
6. Describe the problem in detail.  
7. Select **Raise ticket**.  

You receive a Ticket ID, such as `TKT0042`, and a confirmation email. The support team responds within **48 hours**.

### Troubleshooting Checklist

The ticket form asks you to open these four guides first, because they resolve the most common issues:

- [Maya LED & Sensor Settings](/lorawan/sensors-lorawan/common-lorawan-settings/maya_configuration_led_status_common)  
- [LoRaWAN MacSync RS485](/lorawan/datalogger-lorawan-macsync/macsync_rs485_lorawan_introduction)  
- [WiFi MacSync RS485](/wifi/datalogger-wifi/macsync_rs485_wifi_introduction)  
- [MacTalk DM Document](/mactalk/mactalk-dm/mactalk_dm_document)  

### Track Your Ticket

Your tickets are listed under **Support tickets** on the order page, each with its Ticket ID, status and date.

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| Under review | The ticket is received and the team is reviewing it. |
| In progress | An engineer is working on it. You may see a **Join meeting** link for a Google Meet session and an expected resolution date. |
| Resolved | The issue is fixed. A resolution summary is shown on the ticket. |
| Closed | The ticket is closed. The conversation stays available to read. |

You receive an email at each change of status. Every ticket also has its own **Chat** button, so you can discuss that ticket with the engineer handling it.

## Email Support

Use email if you do not have a Macnman Track account, for RMA requests, or when you need a formal written record.

Send an email to: **support@macnman.com**

Use a clear and descriptive subject line, for example:

- `URGENT – Device not joining LoRaWAN network – MacSync-LWSX5 – Site Mumbai`
- `RMA Request – MacSync-LW-X2 – Optical Rainfall Sensor`
- `Configuration Help – MacLink Gateway – ChirpStack Integration`

Include the details listed under [Before You Contact Support](#before-you-contact-support), along with your company name, contact person and phone number.

Once your email is received, the support team will:

1. Log your case in the ticketing system.  
2. Assign a **Ticket ID / Reference Number**.  
3. Share the Ticket ID with you by email.  

Always mention this Ticket ID in follow-up emails so that the team can track the history and avoid duplication.

## Purchased Through a Distributor

If you purchased the product through an authorized distributor, you may raise the issue with that partner. They will consolidate the information and escalate the case to Macnman support if required.

## What Happens Next

### Remote Diagnosis

Depending on the issue, Macnman support may:

- Request additional logs, screenshots or configuration files  
- Ask you to perform certain tests, such as swapping the power supply, changing the antenna or moving the device  
- Schedule a Google Meet session for deeper debugging  

In some cases, support may also request:

- Network server screenshots  
- Gateway logs  
- Field photos of the installation, including the enclosure, cable glands, antenna and orientation  

### Resolution or RMA Guidance

Based on the diagnosis, the support team will either:

- Provide configuration or firmware-related fixes  
- Suggest corrective actions in the field  
- Confirm whether the issue falls under warranty or is a non-warranty case  
- Guide you through the **RMA process** if hardware repair or replacement is required  

If an RMA is needed, you will be asked to ship the product to Macnman as described in the [Return / Replacement / RMA Policy](./5-rma.md) and the [Warranty Terms & Conditions](./1-warranty-tnc.md).

## Priority and Response Time

Tickets raised in Macnman Track receive a response within 48 hours. Chat and email are handled on a best-effort basis.

Priority is given to:

1. Safety-critical and alarm-related applications  
2. Live production deployments  
3. Pilot/POC systems close to deadlines  

Guaranteed response times are available under the [AMC / Extended Support Policy](./7-standard-amc.md), or may be defined in project-specific or enterprise-level support agreements.

## Best Practices for Faster Resolution

- Always include the **product model, serial number and site name** in your first message.  
- Attach clear photos of wiring, antenna placement and the installation environment.  
- Mention any recent changes, such as new firmware, a new network server or a new configuration.  
- Keep one issue in one ticket or chat instead of opening several threads for the same problem.
