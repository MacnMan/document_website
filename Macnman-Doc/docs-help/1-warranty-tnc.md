# Standard Limited Warranty Terms & Conditions

*Effective for products invoiced on or after 10 October 2026.*

Macnman Technologies Pvt. Ltd. (“Macnman”) builds industrial IoT devices for monitoring, automation and long-term field operation. This Standard Limited Warranty is the only warranty Macnman gives on its products. It sets out what is covered, what is not, and how to make a claim.

By purchasing, installing or using a Macnman product, the customer accepts these terms.

## Warranty at a Glance

| Item | Warranty period |
|---|---|
| Macnman hardware: enclosure, PCB, electronics, RF and communication modules | 2 years |
| Optical, ultrasonic and other detachable sensor probes | 12 months |

## 2. Warranty Coverage

### Hardware Warranty (2 Years)

Macnman warrants that its hardware will be free from defects in materials and workmanship for **two (2) years from the date of Macnman’s invoice**, when installed and operated as described in the product’s user manual and datasheet.

The hardware warranty covers:
- Mechanical enclosures  
- PCB and internal hardware assemblies  
- Electronic components  
- RF circuitry and communication modules  

This warranty covers manufacturing defects only.

### Batteries and Sensor Probes

Batteries and sensor probes are excluded from the 2-year hardware warranty. They are covered for the shorter periods below, counted from the same invoice date:
- Optical, ultrasonic and other detachable sensor probes: 12 months  
- Gas sensing elements and cartridges (such as H₂S, NH₃, CO, CO₂, CH₄ and O₂): 6 months  
- Soil probes: 6 months  
- pH probes: 6 months  
- Rechargeable batteries: 6 months  
- Non-rechargeable batteries: no warranty  

Where a product’s datasheet states a different period for one of these items, the datasheet period applies.

The following are normal behaviour and are not defects:
- Gradual loss of battery capacity, and battery life shorter than the datasheet estimate  
- Sensor drift, loss of sensitivity, and the ageing or depletion of a sensing element  
- The need for periodic cleaning, recalibration or replacement of a probe  

### Embedded Firmware

Firmware installed by Macnman is supplied “as is”. Macnman does not warrant that firmware will be uninterrupted, error-free or suited to a particular application.

If the firmware shipped with a product stops the hardware from working materially as its datasheet describes during the hardware warranty period, Macnman’s only obligation is to make reasonable efforts to provide a corrected version.

### Repaired or Replaced Products

A repair or replacement does not extend or restart the warranty. A repaired or replaced product or part is covered only for the remainder of the original warranty period.

### How the Warranty Period Is Counted

- The period starts on the date of Macnman’s invoice to its direct customer. For products bought through a distributor, this is Macnman’s invoice to that distributor.  
- The period is not extended by storage, late installation, non-use, time in transit or time spent in the RMA process.  
- A claim must be reported to Macnman before the period ends.

## 3. Remedies

If Macnman’s inspection confirms a defect covered by this warranty, Macnman will, at its sole option:
- Repair the product, using new or refurbished parts  
- Replace it with a new or refurbished product of equivalent function, which may be a newer compatible model  
- Refund the price paid to Macnman for the affected unit, less reasonable depreciation for use, where repair or replacement is not reasonably practical  

Replaced products and parts become the property of Macnman. Service is subject to component availability.

Warranty service is carried out at Macnman’s service centre. It does not include:
- Removal of the product from site, or reinstallation  
- Site visits, travel, access equipment or labour at the installation site  
- Re-commissioning, reconfiguration or recalibration after return  
- The cost of any substitute product or service  

On-site service is available on request at Macnman’s prevailing rates.

**These remedies are the customer’s sole and exclusive remedies for any defect in a Macnman product.**

## 5. After-Sales Service & RMA Handling

### Conditions for a Claim

Macnman accepts a warranty claim only when all of the following are met:
- The defect is reported in writing to **support@macnman.com** within the warranty period, and within 15 days of the defect first appearing  
- Valid proof of purchase is provided  
- The serial number label, QR code and warranty seal are intact and legible  
- The product has been paid for in full. Macnman may suspend warranty service while any payment for the product is overdue, and the warranty period continues to run during that time  
- An RMA Number has been issued by Macnman before the product is shipped  

Products sent without a valid RMA Number may be refused and returned at the customer’s cost. An RMA Number is valid for 30 days from the date of issue.

### RMA Request and Approval

Required details:
- Product model  
- Serial number / QR code / MAC ID  
- Purchase invoice  
- Fault description  
- Installation images (if applicable)  
- Logs or data (if applicable)  

Before shipping, the customer must back up all data and settings and remove SIM cards, memory cards and accessories that were not requested. Macnman may reset a returned product to factory settings and update its firmware, and is not responsible for data, SIM cards or accessories sent with it.

### Shipping & Logistics

- **Customer → Macnman:** The customer bears shipping, packing, insurance and handling. Risk of loss or damage stays with the customer until Macnman receives the product.  
- **Macnman → Customer:** For approved warranty claims, Macnman pays standard return shipping within India. For destinations outside India, the customer bears international freight, insurance, customs duties and taxes in both directions.  
- **All other cases:** For out-of-warranty, excluded and no-fault-found cases, the customer pays shipping in both directions.  

### Inspection & Service

Macnman inspects every returned product. Macnman’s findings on the cause of a failure, and on whether it is covered by this warranty, are final.

If no fault is found, or the failure is not covered, Macnman will return the product unrepaired or offer a paid repair. A diagnostic fee may apply.

If the customer does not approve a quotation, make payment or arrange return within 60 days of Macnman’s written notice, Macnman may recycle or dispose of the product without liability.

### Out-of-Warranty Repair Charges

The repair cost will be communicated in advance and approved by the customer before repairs begin. Parts replaced in a paid repair are warranted for 90 days from the date of return shipment. The rest of the product is not.

The full return procedure is described in the [Return / Replacement / RMA Policy](./5-rma.md).

## 6. Warranty Exclusions

### What Is Not Covered

The warranty does NOT cover:
- Accidental or physical damage, including drops, impact and excessive vibration  
- Cosmetic damage that does not affect function, such as scratches, dents, fading, UV discolouration and label wear  
- Operation or storage outside the datasheet limits for temperature, humidity, pressure, supply voltage or load  
- Incorrect power supply, reversed polarity, over-voltage, surge, electrostatic discharge, lightning or faulty earthing  
- Water or dust ingress caused by incorrect gland, cable entry or sealing, or by an opened enclosure. The ingress protection rating applies only when glands, seals and covers are fitted as the manual describes  
- Damage from chemicals, fertilizers, salt, corrosive materials, insects or rodents  
- Improper installation, wiring or mounting, misuse, neglect, or use other than as intended  
- Use of unofficial accessories, antennas, batteries or power adapters  
- Software, platforms, network servers, gateways, SIM or carrier services, and network coverage not provided by Macnman  
- Damage due to incorrect firmware flashing, or failure to install an update that Macnman has advised  
- Wear-and-tear and consumable items such as cables, connectors, glands, seals, desiccant and mounting hardware  
- Loss or damage in transit, including damage from inadequate packaging on return  
- Theft, vandalism, loss, and force majeure events such as fire, flood, storm and earthquake  
- Use in life-support, safety-critical or other applications where failure could cause injury, death or environmental harm  

Wireless range, battery life, accuracy and similar figures in Macnman datasheets are typical values measured under stated test conditions. Results at a customer site depend on the installation and environment, and a variation from those figures is not a defect.

### What Voids the Warranty

The warranty on a product ends immediately if:
- The product is opened, modified, repaired or serviced by anyone other than Macnman or a person Macnman has authorized in writing  
- A warranty seal is broken or tampered with  
- The serial number label or QR code is removed, altered or made illegible  
- Non-Macnman firmware is installed, or the bootloader or security protections are altered
